## Rally Information

*   Rally registration will take place Friday at [NE Yacht Club](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CoAcXqLMgReeeAjq6) starting at 2 PM
*   Friday night ride will take off from [NE Yacht Club](https://maps.app.goo.gl/CoAcXqLMgReeeAjq6) at 7 PM and will return back to the Yacht Club after a River bonfire
*   The Saturday long ride will depart from [Boom Island Park](https://maps.app.goo.gl/wZ5VHqcbe7GB9SSt8) at 11 AM with breakfast being served at 9:30 AM
*   The Saturday raffle and party will start at 7pm at the [GiantValley American](https://maps.app.goo.gl/DJN5W19XHYqaud6r8) Legion with food and beer being provided to rally participants at 7:30 PM
*   [Facebook Event](https://www.facebook.com/events/641505322127376/?ti=ls)
