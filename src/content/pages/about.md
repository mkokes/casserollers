The Casserollers are a Minneapolis based moped gang, we like to go on weekly rides and do moped related things.

The best way to get in touch with us or meet up for a ride would be via the [Minnesota Moped Riders](https://www.facebook.com/groups/mnmopedriders/) facebook group. We try to announce all the where’s and when’s of the ride details there.

Follow us on Instagram at [@thecasserollers](https://www.instagram.com/thecasserollers/) for rally photos and news.

To sign up for our text service to be alerted when weekly rides are announced, text JOIN to [+18335108834](sms:+18335108834?&body=JOIN).
