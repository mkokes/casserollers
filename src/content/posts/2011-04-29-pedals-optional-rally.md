Tomorrow at the Hexagon Bar! Pray for good weather!

[![Pedals Optional Rally ](http://mplsmopeds.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/211162_199013633462982_2324539_n.jpeg)](http://mplsmopeds.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/04/211162_199013633462982_2324539_n.jpeg)

> Schell’s Brewing, Autocycle Twin Cities, and Brown Dog present Pedals Optional’s Spring Opener!
> 
> Hexagon Bar, located just off of the Greenway, is hosting a full day, outdoor event including swap meet, bike show, raffle, rally, live music, and FUN!
> 
> Local Bands include: 20 Dollar Love, Pennyroyal, Ponyboy, Crankshaft, Aloha from Hell, Hardcore Crayons, and John Hooks & the Latter Day Crooks.
> 
> Any alternative transportation is welcome, the focus of the rally being bicycles of all types (vintage, new, custom, homemade, rat, fixed, whatever you got!), and AutoBicycles, (bicycles with engine kits) up to 100cc. Autocycles are planning a ride around town prior to the event, contact Tony for more information (logan.tony1@gmail.com)
> 
> \*\*just in! Photography contest goes along with the event! Prizes awarded! Photos submitted used for future promotion, and photographers get sweet cred if they are used. Wanna know more? holla!\*\*
> 
> Vendor & Swap meet is being organized, reserve a space for $15! Contact Tina (tinaraneaschauer@gmail.com)
> 
> Trophies are being made for a variety of categories of bikes being shown. Show yours for $3, and compete for sweet trophies and glory!
> 
> TONS of raffle prizes are being donated from local bike friendly organizations, including Pizza Luce,North Star Roller Girls, Schell’s Brewing, Tired Ol’ Belts, Davanni’s, Adam Turman, Extreme Noise, Birchwood Cafe, Nice Ride, MSP Bike Posters, Longfellow Grill, and many, many more! Donations still being accepted.
> 
> The event is FREE, all ages, everybody is welcome.
