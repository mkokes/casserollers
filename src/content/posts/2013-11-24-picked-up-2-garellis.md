I picked up a VIP and  Super Sport. Both in decent condition. 

I started work on the VIP and got it running well. It takes off with the 2 speed and brings me all the way up to 31 mph. I am re upholstering the seat right now and will clean up the rest of the bike soon. 

I got it started but it ran light crap. I thought it might have bad gas and when i drained the tank it came out BLACK. After a tank flush and a carb clean she started and ran great.
