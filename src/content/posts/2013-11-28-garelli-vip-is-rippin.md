The VIP I picked up a week ago is rippin. I had to change the gas, and oil, fill tires, make a new seat, lube throttle cable, fix intake, and tighten all of the nuts and bolts. Every single one was loose. 

Now its ridding very well and goes 32 mph. Its a smooth 2 speed and is very comfortable.
