Lots going on this week aside from profuse sweating.

On Wednesday we’ll be having our weekly ride. Same deal as always meet up at [Pizza Luce in Uptown](http://www.pizzaluce.com/locations/?loc=uptown "Pizza Luce in  Uptown") around 6pm.

[![](http://mplsmopeds.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/07/eWotDjU0kraWn8-640m-300x225.jpg)](http://mplsmopeds.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/07/eWotDjU0kraWn8-640m.jpeg)

On Thursday, [Blue Cat Motors](http://bluecatmotors.com/ "Blue Cat Motors") will be putting on ‘Third Thursday’ their monthly motorcycle/moped/scooter meetup.

[![](http://mplsmopeds.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/bluecatmotors.gif)](http://mplsmopeds.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/05/bluecatmotors.gif)

Saturday we’ll have another ride meet up around 11:30 we’re thinking either [Chris and Rob’s](http://www.chrisandrobs.com/ "Chris and Rob's") or at Minnehaha Falls(I’ll post an update when we know more). After that we’ll be shooting a promo video at various different sites then we’ll be riding up to the [Bearded Lady Motorcycle Freak Show](http://www.beardedladymotorcycleshow.com/ "Bearded Lady Motorcycle Freak Show"). It should be a pretty good time
