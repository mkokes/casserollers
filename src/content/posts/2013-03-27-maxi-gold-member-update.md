Mounted the SSR forks and the Mike T disc brake. The disc brake could not be mounted with the original hardware because the nuts hit the caliper mount on the forks. I had to re-drill and tap the disc brake ring adapter for some counter sunk allen bolts. After that it was almost all good. Then i noticed the brake rotor hit a caliper bolt, so I needed to stick the whole disk in a lathe and turn town the outer edge be about 4mm to clear the disc. After all fo that it works great!
