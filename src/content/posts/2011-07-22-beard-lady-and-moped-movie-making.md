Saturday we’ll be meating (not misspelt) up around 11:30 am at [Chris and Rob’s Hot Dog Place](http://www.chrisandrobs.com/ "Chris and Rob's Authentic Hot Dog Place") in south minneapolis.

Here’s the address…  
3101 East 42nd Street  
Minneapolis, MN 55406  
[Link to Google Maps](http://maps.google.com/maps?q=3101+East+42nd+Street,+Minneapolis&gl=us&z=16 "Chris and Robs Address")

From there Capt. Kim and Dallas wanna make a movie or a video or something. They’ve got a couple a spots they’ve got picked out and my guess is when this is all done it’ll receive an NC-17 rating.

After that we’ll be heading up to the [Bearded Lady Motorcycle Freak](http://www.beardedladymotorcycleshow.com/ "Bearded Lady Motorcycle Freak Show") Show to enjoy the festivities. I’d say we’ll probably end up rolling in around 2:30 or so. So if you can’t make it earlier for the ride you can still meet up with us there.

It’s at 13th ave and Univeristy, right by [erte](http://www.ertedining.com/ "erte") and the [331 club](http://www.331.mn/home.php "331 Club")
