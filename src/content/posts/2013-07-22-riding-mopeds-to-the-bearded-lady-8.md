There’s this motorcycle show in Minneapolis that we try to make it to every year. It’s called the bearded lady and it’s kind of a conglomeration of every type of two wheeled vehicle you can imagine. We (The Casserollers) try to make it every year and do a ride of some sort. This year we blasted some of the parkways around Minneapolis and Lake Calhoun before going to the show. It was good times and one of our Members, Jake Cain took first place in the motorcycle category with his Tomos Targa Lx that he just finished rebuilding after an accident with a car last year.
