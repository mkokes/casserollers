I got 5 bells done! 3 Puch E50s and 2 Minarelli V1 bells with puch gears.

The Minarelli bell is much smaller and stronger than a Puch bell. This way you can run a KTM or a V1 Hammer clutch in a Puch engine. It will be lighter and have less rotating mass. Spool it up fast!
